# Delivery Policy

## Delivery Date

The expected delivery date should be agreed upon before the project begins.

Delivery estimates may change when the client requests additional work, provides required information late, or changes the original scope.

## Client Materials

The client is responsible for providing information, assets, approvals, and other materials required for the creator to complete the project.

Delays in receiving required materials may affect the delivery date.

## Final Delivery

Final deliverables should be provided according to the format and delivery method agreed upon by the parties.

The creator should confirm that the client has received the final deliverables when practical.

## Late Delivery

If the creator expects a significant delay, the creator should inform the client and provide an updated estimated delivery date.

The parties may agree to modify the project schedule when circumstances outside the original plan affect delivery.

## Acceptance

The client should review delivered work within the review period specified in the project agreement.

Acceptance requirements should be agreed upon before work begins when the project requires formal approval.
