# Payment Policy

## Payment Terms

The client and creator should agree on the project price, currency, payment schedule, and payment method before work begins.

The agreed payment terms should be recorded in the project agreement.

## Deposits

A project may require an upfront deposit before work begins.

The deposit amount and whether it is refundable should be clearly agreed upon before the project starts.

## Outstanding Payments

Creators may pause delivery of unfinished work when an agreed payment is overdue, subject to the terms of the project agreement.

The creator should communicate the outstanding amount and payment status to the client before pausing work.

## Additional Work

Work outside the agreed project scope may require an additional fee.

The creator should confirm the additional cost with the client before beginning substantial additional work.

## Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on the agreed project terms and the amount of work already completed.

Any refund policy should be communicated clearly before the project begins.
