# Cancellation Policy

## Client Cancellation

A client may request cancellation of a project before completion.

The financial consequences of cancellation should depend on the project agreement, the amount of work already completed, and any agreed deposit or cancellation terms.

## Creator Cancellation

A creator may request cancellation when circumstances prevent the creator from reasonably completing the project.

The creator should communicate the situation to the client as soon as reasonably possible.

## Work Already Completed

When a project is cancelled, the parties should account for work already completed and any non-refundable costs that were agreed upon before the project began.

## Refunds After Cancellation

Any refund should be determined according to the agreed cancellation terms.

If the project agreement does not clearly address cancellation, the parties should attempt to reach a reasonable agreement based on work completed and payments already made.
