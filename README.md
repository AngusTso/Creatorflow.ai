# CreatorFlow AI

A small set of free tools for creators to handle the client-communication side of the job.
No account needed — you always review the final answer before a client sees it.

**Live:** https://creatorflow-ai-bice.vercel.app/

## Features

- **Quote Assistant** — describe a project in plain words; the AI estimates the hours and a small
  calculator turns them into a transparent quote. The AI never guesses the final price.
- **Contract / TOS Assistant** — turn your project and usual terms into a first draft of business
  terms, grounded in your own policy knowledge base. *(Coming soon.)*
- **Script Analyzer** — paste a voice script and get a structured read: summary, tone, emotion,
  vocal difficulty and voice direction.

## Note

AI output is a draft for your own review — it is not legal, financial or professional advice.
